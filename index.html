About me

Data scientist focused on end-to-end data pipelines (ingestion, ETL, data quality and orchestration) that feed machine learning models and analytics. Currently in R&D at Samsung Research Brazil; previously spent eight years at Ypê bringing pipelines, machine learning and simulation to manufacturing and sales. M.Sc. in Production Engineering and Operations Research from the Federal University of Santa Catarina. Python, SQL, Spark, Databricks, Airflow and dbt.
